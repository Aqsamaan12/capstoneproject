A prestigious, forward-thinking event that brings together global leaders,
entrepreneurs, innovators, and investors to explore and showcase the latest

2024-11-15(THU) ~ 18(FRI)

@Javits Center 429 11th Ave, New York, NY 10001, United States

Main Program

Summit Dinner

The Gala Dinner provides a formal networking opportunity and includes presentations like the Global Competitiveness Award.

Annual Meeting

This gathering is exclusive to the Global Federation of Competitiveness Councils (GFCC) members and allows for in-person connections.

Field Trips Workshop

These immersive sessions offer participants firsthand insights into the innovation ecosystems of the host country, exploring local talent.

Key Themes

The event will focus on topics such as Green Transition, Digitalization, Smart Cities and Urban Innovation, Circular Economy, and Sustainability.

B2B Matchmaking

To help attendees find potential partners for international projects, particularly within the Eureka Network.
